# How playbacks are measured

The plugin turns Jellyfin's playback events into one record per play. This page describes the exact rules.

## Which events count

The plugin listens to `PlaybackStart`, `PlaybackProgress` and `PlaybackStopped`. An event is ignored when:

- it has no item or no user;
- it is **automated** (progress generated by the server, not reported by a client);
- the item is an **extra** (trailer, featurette…) or **theme media**.

## One play, one key

A play is identified by its `PlaySessionId`, or `DeviceId | UserId | ItemId` when the client sends none. A *Stop* without a session id is matched to the open play of the same device, user and item.

| Situation | Result |
|---|---|
| *Start* for a new key | A new play opens immediately (no waiting delay). |
| *Start* on a device where the same user has another play open | The previous play is closed at that moment (auto-play of the next episode, for example). |
| *Start* again for the same key and item | Treated as progress: the play is not split. |
| Event for a key closed less than 2 min ago | Ignored (late events). |
| No event for **5 min** while playing | The play is closed at its last event. |
| No event for **60 min** while paused | The play is closed at its last event. |

## The state machine

Each event is applied in arrival order. With `dt` = real time since the previous event and `dp` = change of position:

```txt
if the player was playing:
    WatchedMs += dp unknown ? dt : min(dt, max(dp, 0) + 2 s)
if the player was paused:
    PausedMs  += dt

expected = real time since the last known position while playing, 0 while paused
if dp > expected + 5 s:   SeekCount += 1, SkippedMs += dp - expected   # forward seek
if dp < -5 s:             SeekCount += 1                               # backward seek

playing -> paused (not on Stop): PauseCount += 1
WatchedMs is always capped at the real time since the start.
```

Consequences:

- **watched time never exceeds the real time**, and never exceeds how far the position moved (+2 s margin);
- a pause, buffering or a backward seek adds nothing;
- a forward seek is counted with the time skipped (this is how intro skipping is detected);
- a client that pauses right before stopping does not add a pause.

## Tracks and transcoding

- **Audio and subtitle tracks** are read on start and on every progress event. Every change is logged in `StreamChanges` (`at`, `kind`, `index`, `language`). Watched time is also counted per audio track: at the end, the track watched the longest becomes the play's audio track and language.
- **Transcoding** (from Jellyfin's `TranscodingInfo`), refreshed on every progress: play method, source and target video codecs, source and target audio codecs, video and audio direct flags, output width, height, bitrate and framerate, hardware acceleration, transcode reasons, video range (SDR, HDR10, Dolby Vision…) and source resolution.
- **Network**: `IsRemote` from the client IP. Loopback, private IPv4 ranges (10/8, 172.16/12, 192.168/16), link-local, IPv6 ULA and site-local are local; anything else is remote; unknown when there is no address.

## End of a play

- On *Stop*: the end time, the final position and Jellyfin's `PlayedToCompletion` flag are recorded.
- When the play is closed by the time-outs above or at start-up, `PlayedToCompletion` stays unknown and the end is the last event.

## Saving and crashes

- Open plays are saved every **15 seconds** (checkpoint) and closed plays immediately.
- On a clean shutdown the plays in progress are saved as open.
- At start-up, every play still open is closed at its last checkpoint: **at most about 15 seconds are lost** after a crash.
- Writes go through a single background queue; one action is retried once if it fails.
