# Libraries and media

## Libraries

![Libraries](/screenshots/en/libraries.jpg){.shot}

One card per Jellyfin library (movies, shows, music) with its size, watch time, views and viewers.

## A library

![Library items](/screenshots/en/library.jpg){.shot}

- KPIs: watch time, views (with viewers), **never watched** (share of the catalog), average completion.
- **Items** tab: search, table or poster grid, sortable by name, views, viewers or watch time, with a 14-day sparkline. Series libraries add *Retention S1E1 → end*.
- **Activity** tab: the play history of the library.

The **Statistics** tab:

![Library statistics](/screenshots/en/library-stats.jpg){.shot}

- Genres, play method, **time to watch** (days from addition to first view).
- **Delay from addition to first view** (plugin): histogram from < 1 h to > 4 weeks.
- **Decades watched vs catalog**, **growth vs consumption** (added, first watched and, with the plugin, removed), and the **never watched** list.

## Movie

![Movie](/screenshots/en/movie.jpg){.shot}

- Views (and clicks), viewers, **finished views**, transcoded share.
- **Completion funnel**: clicks → views (≥ 30 s) → reached half → finished (≥ 90 %).
- **Where people stop**: histogram of stop positions.
- **Sittings to finish the movie**: once, twice, three times, four or more.
- **Marked as played** (plugin): by hand, at the end of playback, or other, per user.
- Tabs: viewers and history.

## Series and episodes

![Series](/screenshots/en/series.jpg){.shot}

- Watch time, views, clicks (share of clicks without a view), **retention** (S1E1 → last episode), **binges**.
- **Retention per episode**: unique viewers of each episode; click to open it.
- **Forward skips** (plugin): short skips at the start (probably the intro), plays with a skip, average time skipped.
- **Episodes** tab per season: views, viewers, average completion, **drop-offs**, intro skips.
- **Cadence between episodes** and **release → watch delay**.

![Episode](/screenshots/en/episode.jpg){.shot}

An episode page has previous/next buttons, a funnel, **stop positions** (the final peak is the end credits), pauses per view, skips and marks.

## Artists and albums

![Artist](/screenshots/en/artist.jpg){.shot}

- Listens, listening time, listeners, **skip rate** (clicks without a 30 s listen).
- **Top tracks** with skips per track, album tiles, and **skips by hour**.

![Album with the track panel](/screenshots/en/album.jpg){.shot}

An album shows its tracks, **full listens** (every track on the same day) and, when you click a track, a side panel with its listens, skips, listeners and latest listens.
