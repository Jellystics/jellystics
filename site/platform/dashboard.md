# Overview, live and history

## Navigation

- The sidebar groups pages in **Observe** (Overview, Live, History), **Explore** (Libraries, Users) and **Understand** (Insights, Discover, Playback). Live shows a red dot when something is playing.
- The **period picker** (top bar) offers Today, Last 7/30/90 days, Month to date, Year to date, All time and a custom range. *Compare to previous period* adds deltas to every KPI (relative % for counts, points for ratios).
- The period, filters, tabs and sorting are kept in the URL: any view can be bookmarked or shared.
- **`⌘K` / `Ctrl+K`** opens the command palette: search movies, series, episodes, albums, artists, tracks and users, jump to any page, start a sync or toggle the theme.
- The **user menu** holds the theme, the language, the settings and *Log out*.
- Every chart has a **CSV** button that downloads its data.

## Overview

![Overview](/screenshots/en/overview.jpg){.shot}

- **KPIs**: Watch time, Views, Clicks (each with a sparkline; click one to choose what the main chart shows), Active users, Average completion.
- **{Metric} per day**: the selected metric over the period; with comparison, the previous period is drawn too.
- **All plays**: one dot per play (start time × minutes watched, coloured by type); click a dot to open the title.
- **Plays by library**: views per day (or week) and library.
- **Top content** (all, movies, series, music), **Live** (two current sessions), **Users** (top 8 by watch time).
- **By type**: share of watch time per type, then play method.
- **When**: weekday × hour heatmap. **Peak concurrent streams** per day. **On this day**: what was watched on this date in earlier years.

## Live

![Live](/screenshots/en/live.jpg){.shot}

Updated in real time over a WebSocket.

- **Now playing**, **Transcodes** (with hardware-accelerated count), **Outgoing bitrate** (sum of active streams), **Today's peak**.
- One card per session: artwork, title, user, device, play method, paused badge and progress.
- **Live outgoing bitrate** per stream, **Concurrent streams** today in 15-minute slots, and **Today's timeline**: who watched what and when streams overlapped.

## History

![History table](/screenshots/en/history.jpg){.shot}

- **Filters**: free text, type (movies, series, music), *Views only*, user, client, device, play method. Click a client or device cell to filter on it.
- **Table**: date, user, content, watched, completion, device, play method and View/Click badge. Sort by date, watched time or completion; 25 to 200 rows per page.
- **Export** downloads the whole filtered history as CSV, generated by the server.
