# Users and recaps

## Users

![Users](/screenshots/en/users.jpg){.shot}

- Table of users: watch time, views, split by type, last activity and a 14-day sparkline.
- **User retention**: weekly cohorts, and how many come back the next weeks.
- **Everyone's share over time**: each user's share of watch time week by week.

## A user

![User page](/screenshots/en/user.jpg){.shot}

The **Overview** tab:

- Watch time, views, **active days**, **current streak** (and record), average completion.
- **Activity over 12 months** (calendar heatmap), **In progress** (series started, with the next episode), **Clock** (views per hour).
- **Profile** scores: regularity, discovery, concentration, finished views.
- **Streak & records**, **Devices & versions** (outdated app versions are flagged), **Original or dubbed** (audio and subtitle languages).
- **Logins** (plugin): number, last login and failed attempts per device.
- **Comparison** with another user.

The **History** tab is the history page filtered on this user. The **Taste** tab shows the top genres for series, movies and music, side by side with another user if you pick one:

![Taste comparison](/screenshots/en/user-taste.jpg){.shot}

## Recap

![Yearly recap](/screenshots/en/recap.jpg){.shot}

`/users/<id>/recap/2026` (a year) or `/users/<id>/recap/2026-11` (a month):

- total hours, equivalent days, views and active days;
- top series, movies and artists;
- hours per month or per day, best month or day, **biggest binge**, **longest streak**;
- artists discovered, and a **ranking with change** against the previous period.
